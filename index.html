The Christchurch option

Finish at Mount Cook

Fly into Queenstown and home from Christchurch, and the last few days become a road trip: Wānaka, Lake Pukaki, Aoraki/Mount Cook, then over to Christchurch. You don't double back, and it's about $100 cheaper for the two of you.

    Velocity points

    Worth using here

    17.9kpoints one way, economy

    Virgin flies non-stop to Queenstown from both Brisbane and Melbourne, so it's a straight Velocity reward seat, no partner airline needed. Point Hacks puts it at about 17,900 points one way in economy (35,500 in business), plus taxes. That's roughly 35,800 return each.

    • Against cash fares of $600 to $900 return, that's about 1.7 to 2.5 cents a point, which is a solid use of Velocity.
    • Business is about 71,000 return each and makes a three hour flight very comfortable, if the points are spare.
    • Reward seats are limited, especially late January. Search the exact dates in the Velocity app or on the Virgin site; seats.aero also tracks Velocity availability.
    • The points figure is from Point Hacks, not a live search, so confirm the price and taxes before you book.

    The hiking

    What you can actually walk

    • The Great Walks are mostly booked. The Milford Track sold out in 34 minutes when 2026/27 bookings opened in May. Routeburn and Kepler huts (NZ$132 a night for non-residents) were around 91 to 95% full last peak season, so expect them full in January and watch the DOC site for cancellations. December and late February give you a better shot.
    • No booking needed: Roys Peak and Isthmus Peak near Wānaka (16 km, 5 to 7 hours, 1,200 m of climbing), Ben Lomond above Queenstown (6 to 8 hours, or less from the gondola), Rob Roy Glacier (3 to 4 hours), Key Summit on the Milford Road (about 3 hours), and Gertrude Saddle on a dry day (expert route).
    • Mueller Hut at Mount Cook needs a booking from 11 November to 30 April (NZ$55 a night, no walk ins). Worth trying for, as it's one of the best overnights in the country.
    • Base in Queenstown and hire a car. Wānaka is about an hour away, Te Anau and the Milford Road about two, Mount Cook about three.
    • Fiordland can rain heavily in any month, and the sandflies are always there. Pack for both.
